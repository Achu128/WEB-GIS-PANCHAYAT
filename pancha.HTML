Introduction

About Kalady

Kalady Grama Panchayat is located in Aluva Taluk of Ernakulam district, Kerala. It comes under Angamaly Block Panchayat and is situated near the banks of the Periyar River.

Kalady is historically and culturally significant as the birthplace of Adi Shankaracharya, the renowned Indian philosopher who propagated Advaita Vedanta.

The region represents a combination of cultural heritage, agricultural landscapes, residential settlements and developing urban influences. Its temples, educational institutions and religious centres contribute to its identity.

Geographical Profile

Understanding the Landscape

Kalady's geographical location, river system, climate and settlement pattern influence its environmental and socio-economic characteristics.

01. Location

Located in Aluva Taluk, Ernakulam district, Kerala. It is administered under Angamaly Block Panchayat.

02. Area

The geographical area of the panchayat is approximately 16.684 square kilometres, according to the panchayat portal.

03. River System

The Periyar River is an important geographical feature of the region. It supports agriculture, water resources and local ecosystems.

04. Climate

Kalady experiences a tropical monsoon climate with heavy rainfall during the southwest monsoon season.

05. Relief and Soil

The region consists mainly of relatively low-lying terrain, agricultural fields and settlements with alluvial and lateritic soils in the wider landscape.

06. Connectivity

Kalady is connected to Angamaly and Perumbavoor. Cochin International Airport and Angamaly railway station provide regional connectivity.
